Expense Reimbursement Guidelines - Corporate Finance Department

1. Travel Expenses: Employees can claim reasonable accommodation, transit, and meals during business travel. Economy class flights are mandatory for all domestic travel under 5 hours.
2. Daily Meal Allowance: The maximum daily budget for meals is $75. Receipts must be provided for individual items exceeding $25.
3. Client Entertainment: Entertaining clients requires pre-approval from the department head. Itemized receipts detailing guest names and business purposes are compulsory.
4. Submission Deadlines: All corporate expense claims must be filed through the financial portal within 30 days of incurring the cost. Late submissions face rejection.
5. Corporate Credit Cards: Corporate cards are solely for approved business expenses. Personal use is strictly forbidden and triggers immediate audit reviews.
