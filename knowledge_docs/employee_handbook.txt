Employee Handbook - HR Policy and Core Workplace Guidelines

1. Equal Employment Opportunity: We provide equal opportunities to all employees and applicants without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, or disability.
2. Code of Conduct: All staff must maintain high ethical standards, professional integrity, and respect for colleagues. Discrimination, harassment, or workplace bullying will result in disciplinary action up to termination.
3. Working Hours and Attendance: Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Remote work arrangements must be pre-approved by your direct line manager.
4. Paid Time Off (PTO): Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation annually. Sick leave requires a medical certificate if extending beyond three consecutive working days.
5. Performance Reviews: Evaluations occur semi-annually in June and December to assess goals, provide feedback, and align on career development opportunities.
