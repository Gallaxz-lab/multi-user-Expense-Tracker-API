Building Safety and Facilities Emergency Protocols

1. Fire Evacuation Plan: In case of fire alarms, exit the building immediately using the designated stairwells. Do not use the elevators under any circumstances.
2. Assembly Point: Once outside, proceed directly to the designated muster area in the North Parking Lot. Wait for floor wardens to complete the roll call.
3. First Aid Kits: Emergency medical kits, automated external defibrillators (AEDs), and safety equipment are located near the main reception desk and on every floor elevator lobby.
4. Visitor Access Control: All guests must sign in at the front desk, obtain a temporary visitor badge, and remain accompanied by an employee escort at all times.
5. Hazard Reporting: Report structural defects, spills, broken locks, or lighting failures immediately via the facilities internal ticketing desk portal.
